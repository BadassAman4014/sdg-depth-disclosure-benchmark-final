# Human Coding Protocol: Symbolic vs. Substantive SDG Disclosures

*Based on Legitimacy Theory (Ashforth & Gibbs 1990; Suchman 1995) and De Kok (2025, Management Science)*

---

## 🎯 Task Objective
For each observation in the Golden Dataset, evaluate the provided **Passage** with respect to the matched **Keyword / SDG Category** and assign a classification label:

- **`sym`** (Symbolic Mention)
- **`sub`** (Substantive Mention)

---

## 📖 Operational Definitions

### 1. `sym` — Symbolic Disclosure
Disclosures that express general aspirations, commitments, values, policies, or positive claims **without** verifiable operational implementation, measurable results, allocated budgets, or verified progress.

**Characteristics:**
- **Aspirational / Declarative:** "We strive to...", "We support the UN SDGs...", "Sustainability is in our DNA."
- **Policy & Compliance only:** Listing codes of conduct, compliance guidelines, or legal citations without operational details.
- **Vague intent:** No concrete numbers, timelines, specific budgets, or verified deliverables.

---

### 2. `sub` — Substantive Disclosure
Disclosures that provide specific, verifiable evidence of tangible actions, resource allocations, operational transformations, measurable targets, or audited outcomes.

**Characteristics:**
- **Concrete Projects & Initiatives:** Named programs with specific deployment (e.g., "Installed solar panels across 12 facilities in FY23").
- **Quantified Metrics & KPIs:** Exact numbers, percentages, baseline reductions (e.g., "Reduced Scope 1 emissions by 24.5% compared to 2019").
- **Allocated Resources:** Explicit budgets, dedicated capital expenditures, or specialized teams.
- **Third-Party Verification:** Audits, ISO certifications, or external assurance reports (e.g., "Verified by PwC / Bureau Veritas").

---

## 👥 Inter-Coder Protocol (Coder 1 & Coder 2)
1. **Independent Coding:** Coder 1 and Coder 2 annotate the sample independently without consulting each other.
2. **Reliability Check:** Inter-coder agreement ($\text{Cohen's }\kappa$) is computed. A score of $\kappa \ge 0.80$ indicates strong agreement.
3. **Consensus Resolution:** For cases where Coder 1 and Coder 2 disagree, a third reviewer (or joint consensus discussion) resolves the final `human_consensus` label.
