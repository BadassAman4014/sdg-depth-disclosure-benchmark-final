ACQUISITIONS
CHERRY PICKING ACCRETIVE DEALS
H1 2018 acquisitions of around 900 units at an average multiple of 25x, of which 400 units in London, 
and the remaining units mainly in core locations such as Berlin and Frankfurt. In addition, over 400 
units, which are pre-marketed and newly built, were acquired in London, expected to be marketed and 
rented in the coming quarters, bringing London’s share of the June portfolio to approx. 850 units and 
5% of the portfolio value. GCP used the current market opportunity  to cherry pick accretive 
properties in London following our acquisition criteria.
DISPOSALS
CAPITAL RECYCLING
Portfolio growth was partly offset by disposals and reclassification of non-core properties to 
assets held for sale. Following its capital recycling strategy GCP disposed non-core assets in the 
first half of 2018 at a value of €142 million, generating a profit of €88m over the total cost, 
reflecting a profit margin of 160%. The were disposal created a 12% profit over the net book 
value. After the reporting period, in the third quarter of 2018, GCP executed further disposals 
for approx. €35 million.
